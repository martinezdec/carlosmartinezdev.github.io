About

Built on paper first

Game Designer specialized in Level Design, with shipped titles on Steam and studio work abroad. This portfolio spans professional and independent projects — Shelley Manor, Harmony in the Wild, and my own TRUST.

I mix design, code and art to build prototypes that feel good to move through — ideas that work on paper before they ever touch an engine.
